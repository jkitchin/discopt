(HiGHS's HighsDomain::CutpoolPropagation), DISCOPT_HIGHS_SEARCH_POOL_PROP.

Verdict: H2 FALSIFIED on b-ball. Pool propagation never fires there (0 of 16k cuts ever
pass the capacity test); the pool arm is bit-identical to off. Objective propagation
(HiGHS's ObjectivePropagation, ported but not wired into the search) is the only lever that
moves: -24 % (20k) / -42 % (99999) of the gap, short of the kill line; adding the pool to it
makes the bound worse than objective propagation alone.
